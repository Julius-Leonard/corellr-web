Features

Everything your finance team actually needs

Built by a CFO with 20+ years in the NFP sector. Corellr solves the problems finance teams face every single budget cycle.

👥

Headcount budgeting

Model all people costs with full Australian compliance. Superannuation, MCB capping, salary packaging (PBI & standard), planned hires, and leavers — calculated correctly, automatically.

📋

Opex planning

Budget operating expenses by GL code, cost centre, and funding source. Enter monthly or annual figures, import from CSV, or break down by quarter. Supports multiple funding sources per line.

📊

Consolidated P&L

See your complete budget in one board-ready view — headcount costs and opex combined, with monthly columns, full-year totals, and one-click Excel export.

🎯

Multiple budget scenarios

Model Base Case, Headcount Freeze, Growth, and custom scenarios side-by-side. Each scenario is independent but draws from the same master roster, so comparisons are always apples-to-apples.

🔒

Locked opening position

Approve and lock your budget baseline by department after board sign-off. Track variance between the approved plan and your rolling forecast in real-time throughout the year.

📥

Bulk import & export

Import your employee roster from any HRIS or payroll system via CSV. Preview changes before applying. Export board packs, department reports, and scenario comparisons in Excel format.

How it works

Up and running in an afternoon

No lengthy onboarding, no consultants, no six-month implementation. Most teams have their first budget built in under a day.

1

Import your team

Upload your employee roster from any HRIS or payroll system using our simple CSV template. Corellr maps your data and flags anything that needs attention.

2

Configure your setup

Set up cost centres, funding sources, and salary packaging rules once. Corellr remembers your configuration and applies it automatically across every budget cycle.

3

Build your budget

Model headcount and opex with real-time totals updating as you type. Run scenarios, compare options, and refine until the numbers are right.

4

Share with confidence

Export board-ready reports in one click or share a live view directly with your CEO or audit committee. Lock the approved budget and track actuals throughout the year.
